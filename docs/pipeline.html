Astro-Arc · generation pipeline

How one image gets made

One generation turns a birth chart and today's sky into a wallpaper and a matching desktop palette. At the Ultra preset (gpt-5.4 for writing, gpt-image-2.5-sunburst at high) a fresh day costs about $0.077 and a same-day re-run about $0.066 (measured 2026-10-10: a run that re-drew the day's meaning charged $0.0724): the reading and the day's meaning are cached, the material, the prompt and the picture are remade. The render is most of the cost and most of the time. The panel's cost line projects the real figure for whatever preset is set.

Thirteen steps. Five of them call a language model: the reading, the meaning, the material, the prompt and the melodrama check. This document walks the whole path in order, and every step names the file that runs it, what it reads, what it writes, whether it is cached, and the one knob that changes it — so you can retune this thing without reading any Python.

If you only read one section, read Tweak it without touching code.

The pipeline

Steps 2–8 all live in one file, llm_pipeline.py, and are where the picture is actually decided. Everything else is plumbing. The steps that spend money are marked.

  1. 00

    Decide whether to run at all

    Runs
    astro-arc-generate --if-due
    Cost
    free
    Cache
    —
    Knob
    frequency

    The panel is the scheduler. The bar widget loads it with the shell, so its 5-minute poll runs from login whether or not the panel is ever opened, and it passes --if-due; a manual Regenerate does not. Both take the same flock, so a click can never double-render a period the poll just started. Nothing runs while you are logged out — see the Schedule caption in the panel.

    There used to be a systemd timer as well. On Omarchy the shell is the desktop, so it only ever covered a session with no shell running, and it outlived the plugin: after removal it fired into a deleted script every fifteen minutes.

    "Already generated?" is decided by re-keying last-run.json's generatedAt under the frequency set right now, never by comparing its stored periodKey. That key was filed under whatever frequency was set at the time, so comparing it directly made switching Daily → Monthly start a paid render as the immediate consequence of changing a dropdown.

    Period keys: YYYY-MM-DDTHH · YYYY-MM-DD · ISO %G-W%V · YYYY-MM.

  2. 01

    Compute the chart

    Runs
    astro_engine.py
    Cost
    free
    Cache
    —
    Knob
    birth date, time, place

    Astronomy Engine, vendored as one file, gives natal placements and today's transits to about an arcminute; ephemeris.py adds Placidus houses on top of it. It replaced pyswisseph, which needed compiling on every install, after the two were compared over 1900–2100 and across four birth charts: no sign and no retrograde flag ever differed, and a full year of the pipeline's own outputs moved on only a handful of days where an aspect sat within an arcminute of its orb limit. It emits an arc: the moon sign and phase, the dominant transit, and a texture block — polarity, intensity, exposure, multiplicity — which is the raw material for step 3.

    Hourly has no branch here on purpose: transits barely move in an hour, so an hourly schedule shares the daily arc, and the day's reading and meaning, and re-rolls only the material and the picture.

  3. 02

    Stage 1the reading

    Runs
    llm_pipeline.stage1_interpret
    Cost
    ~$0.0050
    Cache
    per period + chart
    Knob
    stage1Model

    One LLM call turns the chart into a psychological reading in a Jungian frame, plus a one-line distillation and a narrative position. It is told, forcefully, never to use visual language — that firewall is what stops the reading from quietly dictating the picture.

    Cached in pipeline/readings/<periodKey>.json, invalidated by STAGE1_SCHEMA_VERSION. Editing the prompt does not invalidate the cache — bump the schema version, or you will test yesterday's output.

  4. 03

    The dial — composition, without an LLM

    Runs
    llm_pipeline.dial_from_texture
    Cost
    free
    Cache
    —
    Knob
    — always follows the chart

    The day's texture becomes hard numbers and fixed sentences: how many sites (1–3), how many objects (2–3 on a quiet day, 4–6 on a crowded one), the word budget (60–130), which verbs, whether the two registers collide (hard days) or cohere (soft ones), how prominent the highlight is (small and to one side, plainly present, or dominating the frame), in what temper, and how the actor treats the highlight, the objects and the place. None of it says what anything is — that is the charter's job.

    The light comes from here too, from the exposure band, and it is written as a failure condition rather than a mood: a low-exposure day must be night or somewhere daylight does not reach, with at least two thirds of the frame dark; a high-exposure day must be full, even daylight. As moods ("most of the frame is unlit"), neither was obeyed. Mood words are not obeyed; counts and failure conditions are.

    Then the style draws. draw_style_choices takes the dial and fills in what each world says for itself, from arrays in the style's charter. Each draw skips whatever this style drew in its last two runs, and is seeded from the period, the style and the history length, so a sweep is reproducible but a same-day regenerate differs:

    • verbs: 3 process verbs (things happening with no one doing them) from the day's polarity, and an activity (work, rest, play, ritual or travel; none repeats until the others have had a turn) with 3 actor verbs from it. Before this, every style got one fixed list, and spill, feed and bear down sat in a third to a half of all prompts, with 83% of actors at a chore.
    • space: one depth recipe, replacing the fixed "half the frame open" that put far off / distance / beyond into 29 of 33 days. Every style also gets the same WALLPAPER rule: somewhere for the eye to go past the subject.
    • highlight mode: today's kind of wrongness for the highlight.
    • condition: how worn things are (newly made, kept, worn, failing, ruined), weighted toward the far end on hard days. Wear used to be required in five places at once, and every image came back weathered.
    • light: a style's own [light] lines replace the global ones, which assume an Earth sky (cosmic and cyberpunk have their own).

    The draws go into the history entry and are shown on the run page.

    The constants survive as NO_TEXTURE_DIAL, the fallback for a reading with no texture block at all. (A pipelineMode: "legacy" setting that pinned the dial back to them was removed on 2026-09-13.)

  5. 04

    Registers — what the scene is made of

    Runs
    llm_pipeline.pick_registers
    Cost
    free
    Cache
    —
    Knob
    registers.toml

    Each image gets a primary register (the protagonist material) and usually a secondary, from 20 registers in five families: living, mineral, built, fluid, made. On a collide day the secondary comes from a different family; on a cohere day, from the same one. The primary rotates away from anything used in the last 5 days (the first 10 runs simply cycle the list, for coverage).

    Registers are picked before Stage 1.5, because they are one of its inputs. Until 2026-09-26 they were picked after the scene had already been cast, so a register could only reach the prompt as an adjective: with the reading pinned, 20 registers came back as one scene.

    A style can say what each register means in its world, in its [<style>.registers] table; that line goes to steps 6 and 7. A style without one falls back to registers.toml's [guidance], which is empty by design.

  6. 05

    Stage 1.5the meaning

    Runs
    llm_pipeline.stage15_meaning
    Cost
    ~$0.0059
    Cache
    per period + chart + style
    Knob
    stage1Model

    The day's myth. Turns the reading into an archetypal constellation, a movement, a felt quality, and the actor the constellation already contains — who is in this situation, and which of the 11 kinds in actors.toml it is (solitary, crowd, animal, swarm, presence, the dead, none and so on).

    The constellations an install has used recently are passed in as already spent, and so are the last 4 actor kinds. Without that pressure, 83% of days reached for the same archetype and most actors were solitary.

    Jung and Neumann say what kind of situation the day is. A charter's myth field says which traditions its stories come from (Shinto kami and yōkai, loa in hardware, the constellations as beings), and the meaning is named in that world's terms where one fits. The actor is named as who they are, not what they are doing; the activity is drawn later.

    Cached in pipeline/amplifications/<periodKey>.json, invalidated by STAGE15_SCHEMA_VERSION and keyed on a hash of the style's charter, so a regenerate never changes the day's meaning, and editing a charter does. presence is only eligible in a style whose charter says how the numinous takes a body.

  7. 06

    Stage 1.5the material

    Runs
    llm_pipeline.stage15_material
    Cost
    ~$0.0059
    Cache
    never
    Knob
    stage1Model

    The furniture of that myth, built from the day's register: 2–6 concrete objects and one highlight — the thing the eye goes to first. At least half the objects, and the highlight, must be made of the primary register's substance.

    What a highlight is depends on the world. Each charter defines it in its highlight field, and a register line may say what form it takes in that material. Ghibli's is the strange thing nobody remarks on; cyberpunk's is something alive in the machine; cosmic's is a constellation-being's body. Each charter's highlight_modes list the kinds of wrongness it can have, and one is drawn per run. No definition names a size comparison any more: "the size of a barn" put barn into 21 of 33 days. Until 2026-10-09 this was "the anomaly", defined once for every style; the data key is still anomaly.

    The last 6 runs' objects and highlights are passed in as spent, and the day's condition is passed in so the objects do not arrive pre-weathered.

    Never cached, so every run, regenerates included, gets a new register and new objects for the same meaning. That is what keeps same-day regenerates from all looking alike. The highlight is mandatory, and it is never flagged in the prompt with words like "out of place"; naming it out loud made it read as a bolted-on item.

  8. 07

    Stage 2the image prompt

    Runs
    llm_pipeline.stage2_image_prompt
    Cost
    ~$0.0139
    Cache
    never (temp 0.95)
    Knob
    stage2Model, artStyle

    Everything above converges here: the reading, the meaning and its actor, the objects and the highlight with its definition and prominence, both registers with their style lines, the dial's composition brief, the style's world, the concept tags of the last 8 generations to avoid, and any hard-blocked terms.

    Its rules, in short: describe only what is physically there, never what it means; concrete and oddly specific over generically mystical; things must be doing something to each other; each register must contribute a substantial named thing of its own. Colour comes from each scene's own materials and light from the dial — there is no fixed palette for the series. (A natal "visual signature" that tried to fix one was removed on 2026-10-08: with and without it, the prompts differed no more than two draws with it.)

    The style decides the world, not just the look. All seven styles have a charter in styles.toml (era, materials, physics, society, weather, inhabitants, strangeness, the numinous, dread, favoured actors, myth, highlight) and their own brief rules, phrased as failure conditions because that is what Stage 2 obeys. Every style has a signature rule: something in frame that could only exist in this world, and an image that could be relabelled as another style fails. The division Stage 2 is given: the registers decide what things are made of; the world decides what place this is and what lives there. See docs/charter-schema.md for every field. A new style with no charter falls back to ghibli's old rules, kept in one labelled block in llm_pipeline.py.

  9. 08

    The melodrama check

    Runs
    llm_pipeline.caption_check
    Cost
    ~$0.0001
    Cache
    never
    Knob
    captionModel (default gpt-4o-mini)

    A cheap judge files the prompt the way a stock library would and answers three questions: does it show motive, does it take sides, is any face or pose performed for the viewer. Force, struggle and death are allowed; telling the viewer what to feel is not. It was tuned against 22 hand-labelled scenes (melodrama-calibration.toml, 21/21 agreement).

    A failure resamples step 7, up to twice, which costs a chat call rather than a render. If every attempt fails the last prompt ships anyway and the failure is recorded in the run's pipeline-meta (captionCheck, one row per attempt) — a strict judge must never cost the day its image.

  10. 09

    Render

    Runs
    openai_image_gen.py
    Cost
    ~$0.042
    Cache
    —
    Knob
    openaiModel, openaiQuality

    The style suffix is appended — its technique, plus, in the styles that define suffix_figures (ghibli, cyberpunk), how that style draws people when a figure is in frame — and the prompt is rendered at the configured tier, every image, people or not.

    The model is sent moderation: "low" where it accepts it, and a safety refusal is retried up to twice with the identical request. Refusals are not deterministic — the same prompt has been refused twice and accepted once — and a refused request is not billed. Any other error fails at once.

    The run enters history.json only now, after the image exists: a refused or failed render no longer moves the register rotation or the avoided-concepts window on.

    Then it is filed in the archive, ~/.local/share/astro-arc/: a page with the image, the reading and every dial value, the complete theme (so it can be saved or exported later), and a thumbnail. Then the gallery, index.html, is rewritten, newest first. The archive is the user's data, not state, so it lives where removing the plugin cannot touch it. astro-arc-prune-history then drops entries past the retention window, never the newest.

Two traps

Editing a stage prompt does not invalidate its cache

Stage 1 and Stage 1.5's meaning are cached by period and chart. Bump the matching *_SCHEMA_VERSION when you change a prompt, or you will be reading yesterday's answer and concluding your edit did nothing. The material is never cached, so a material-prompt edit shows up on the next run.

A sweep that pins one dial cell overstates a template

sweep.py --cell holds one cell for every sample. That is right for isolating a register, but the cell's own sentences (a soft/mid cell says the actor tends things, in a familiar place) then repeat across every sample and read as a style problem. Production rotates cells daily. When judging variety, rotate cells across registers.

Adding a register is one line in one file

Put its name under the family it is made of in registers.toml and you are done — the rotation list and the family lookup are derived from that. For a world style, add a line for it under [<style>.registers] too, or it reaches that world by name alone.

Change something and measure it

sweep.py --out my-test --registers all --pairs           # every register, one fixed reading
sweep.py --out wording --registers botanical --no-image  # prompts only, cents
sweep.py --out dark --cell hard/hi/4/lo --style cyberpunk # pin one dial cell

sweep.py pins the reading and the dial so the only thing varying is the thing under test. Like production, it draws the day's meaning once and the material per register, renders at the configured tier, and records the melodrama verdict (without resampling, so it shows how often production would). It touches no production state — your history, caches, last run, archive and live theme are left alone. Output lands in state/verification/<name>/ with every prompt, cost, render and a CSV.

Use it. This project's own history records that 2–4-sample spot checks let the same content bug ship three times, and that the one comparison batch which would have caught it was never saved.

Test a fresh install without touching your own

There is no install step, and every path derives from $HOME (the archive from $XDG_DATA_HOME, defaulting under it), so a throwaway home is a genuinely new user in seconds:

FRESH=$(mktemp -d)
mkdir -p $FRESH/.config/omarchy/plugins
cp -a . $FRESH/.config/omarchy/plugins/garrett.astro-arc
HOME=$FRESH $FRESH/.config/omarchy/plugins/garrett.astro-arc/backend/bin/astro-arc-generate

That exercises the real defaults a new user gets and the real first-run paths — no birth data, no archive, no caches — which is where fresh-install bugs live.

Two things it cannot cover, because they are not $HOME-scoped: the QML widget needs the running shell, and secret-tool talks to your real keyring, so a throwaway home still finds your real API key.

What the complexity pass found (2026-09-13)

Removed

  • symbol_map.py — 189 lines of fixed-vocabulary image generation, superseded by the LLM pipeline. Nothing imported it; every remaining mention was prose in a comment.
  • The FIGURE HIERARCHY rule in STAGE2_SYSTEM, which capped how many faces could turn toward the viewer. Removed against measured evidence — forced rows of six and nine equally-sized faces rendered cleanly at 3× zoom — with its provenance kept in a comment saying plainly that the finding is about one image model at one quality tier, so a model swap re-opens the question instead of inheriting the answer.

Fixed — three things a fork would have hit

  • openaiModel and openaiQuality default to "", and jq's // only substitutes for null — so a config where the image model had never been picked passed an empty model name to the API instead of falling back.
  • maxCostPerRun had no setter for its entire life, despite the generate script telling you to raise it. Given one — then removed entirely later the same day, along with the quality bump it existed to gate.
  • A comment in openai_image_gen.py asserted the safety classifier is deterministic and that retrying is always wasted spend. Both are false, measured — and that comment was steering a real decision.

Left alone, on purpose

  • cliches.toml is empty by design. Pre-seeding a blocklist from guesses gives false confidence and decays as the model finds synonyms for whatever you guessed instead.
  • image_gen.py (local Stable Diffusion) is no longer offered in the UI but still works if set by hand.
  • The intrusion/anomaly alias keeps old pipeline-meta-*.json files rendering.

Closed — the four judgment calls, all resolved

  • Period-key logic lived in four implementations. Now one, period_key.py, plus Model.js's — which must exist because QML cannot call Python inside a property binding. period_key.py --selftest checks them against one table of cases, including the ISO week-year edges, and runs the JS through node when node is present.
  • pipelineMode: legacy removed. The dial always follows the chart.
  • The people quality bump removed, and with it maxCostPerRun, two cost estimator CLI shims, and ~73 lines of budget projection.
  • imageBackend now defaults to "openai", matching provider.
